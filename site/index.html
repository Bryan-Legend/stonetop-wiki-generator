Stonetop Adventure Sheets

Table-ready adventure sites for Stonetop, Jeremy Strandberg's Powered-by-the-Apocalypse game about a small, isolated village at the edge of the great wood. Each sheet is a single page a GM can run from directly — keyed locations, stat blocks, and clickable HP tracks.

  • Vasilya's Grove A corrupted seed deep in the Great Wood, east of the Stream. Pointcrawl.
  • Underfalls A working Rime Lord shrine in a hanging valley under the ice falls. Ten nodes, one major arcanum.
  • The Sealed Cave A bricked-over mouth in the hills above Stonetop, and the peacebonded gallery behind it.
Built with stonetop-wiki-generator, an open-source static-site generator for Stonetop campaign material.
