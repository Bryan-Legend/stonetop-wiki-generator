# Settled terms — Playbooks & Inserts chapter

One rendering per term, per language, everywhere (see `GLOSSARY.md` for the
policy). This file fixes the vocabulary that recurs across the 21 pages of
Book I's *Playbooks & Inserts* chapter. Extend it as more chapters are
translated; never re-decide a term that is already here.

Rules that hold in **every** language:

- **Stat abbreviations stay English** — STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA — in
  running text ("roll +WIS" → „würfle +WIS"), on the roll buttons, everywhere.
  The dice toast and the sync plumbing key on them, and the likeliest reader
  plays from the English PDF. Full stat *names* are translated.
- **HP and XP stay as-is** in every language (the wiki's chrome and stat
  blocks use them untranslated). "Level", "Damage", "Armor" are translated.
- Slugs, section ids, `data-*` attributes, field keys: never translated.
- Settlement proper names **transliterate** in Cyrillic/kana/hangul
  (Стоунтоп, マーシュエッジ) and go semantic in Chinese; geographic features and
  epithets in plain speech **translate** everywhere (Край Света, 世界の果て).
  This is the operative reading of the GLOSSARY name table, per the settled
  World's End / Golden Oak / Marshedge precedents.
- Personal-name lists (character name suggestions) stay Latin in Latin-script
  languages, transliterate in ru/uk/ja/ko, transcribe phonetically in zh.
- Deity and invented names (Danu, Helior, Aratis, Tor, Crinwin, Lygos,
  Manmarch, suarachan…) keep their forms in Latin scripts, transliterate
  elsewhere.

Move-name table order, all languages: Defy Danger · Struggle as One ·
Let Fly · Clash · Defend · Know Things · Seek Insight · Persuade ·
Aid or Interfere · Make Camp · Forage · Recover · Convalesce ·
Trade & Barter · Have What You Need · Outfit · Requisition · Muster ·
