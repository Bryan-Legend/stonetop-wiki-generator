Fomoraij

The Green Lords damned themselves. Oh, they'll tell you they had no other choice. That their treacherous servants drove them to it. That they knew not the cost. That they were lied to by the Things Below. But, no, they knew what they were doing. They just didn't care.

And why would they have cared? Had they not lost everything? Had they not been driven from their homes and hunted like beasts, by the very servants they had created? By those who owed them everything?

So, yes damn you, the Green Lords made their deals. They called up the darkness and the poison. Let it flow through their minds. Their flesh. Their lands.

And when not even that was enough to turn the tides, when they were betrayed again—this time by their fellow Makers—and they were corralled into one last trap… well, what did they care?

What could they care? Let the traitors have the surface. For now.

And so, twisted and mad with hatred, they went below. Into the bosom of their masters. There, they wait. They spawn new horrors. They hate.

And one day, perhaps, they will return.

Names

Slavic-sounding, but with more vowels and softer consonants: Aawnaswasiya, Blaagu, Doogromil, Eleema, Loobimoor, Miwooslawa, Oolliyanaw, Rabomoor, Weelimir, Yoanaw

Hooks

  • Infected servants are watching villagers near the Stream
  • An earthquake opens a crack in a cave bear's den, exposing them to a Fomoraij's lair and influence
  • Hunters/trappers go missing, then turn up weeks later horribly mutated
  • A Fomoraij's minions steal children from their beds and drag them to their master
  • Forest Folk glyphs show people filing into a cave, laden with gifts (for the Fomoraij that secretly entranced them)

Dwellers beneath

The Fomoraij have dwelt beneath the Great Wood for centuries. Their influence can be felt, certainly, and they sometimes send forth minions, but they have not seen the surface since the fall of Vor Svetelik.

To create a Fomoraij, start with 2 themes from the Things Below.

Combine with a constraint that keeps it from emerging, the nature of its lair, and 2-3 forms. Give it a name and perhaps an ominous title. Write it up as a threat (a villain, a beast, or a magical entity). If the PCs are likely to fight it, also write it up as a monster (see example on "Typical" Fomoraij).

Constraint
1-2Confinement: by accident, by artifice, by its own size or nature (Instinct to capture others, to escape)
3-4Disdain: of the world above, of the world that betrayed them (Instinct to punish anything that reminds it of its past)
5-6Decay: of its mind, its power, its sense of self (Instinct to lash out)
7-8Fear: of the Fae, of the Makers, of the much-hated light (Instinct to see threats everywhere)
9-10Obsession: with hoarding, experimenting, digging, etc., to the exclusion of all else (Instinct to pursue its obsession)
11-12Patience: it waits, it gathers power, it makes preparations (Instinct to further its plans)
Lair
1A yawning cavern, flooded; dotted with islands and unlikely structures
2Endless rough-hewn tunnels, like an ant hive
3An inverted forest of roots, tubers, fungus hanging from the ceiling
4A series of underground streams, rivers, lakes, all teeming with horrors
5A deep, vertical shaft ending at a vault of white stone
6Tunnels and chambers, every inch lined with bones
7The petrified belly of a gargantuan, primordial titan
8Vaulted tunnels lined with roots of iron-infused wood, rusted and bleeding
9Limestone caverns, the walls pulsing with red crystal
10Limestone caves and columns, wet and dripping, festooned with slimy life
11Strangely smooth tunnels coated in warm, pulsing organic material
12A vast chamber of white stone, a mighty dead tree growing down from the ceiling Fomoraij lairs are always found underground and beneath the Great Wood, accessed via caves and Green Lord ruins. The sites above are often corrupted by the Things Below.
Form
1A swarm of smaller things/a hive-mind consciousness
2Boneless/squeezing/amorphous
3Fungal/lichenous/mossy
4Insectoid/shelled
5Too many limbs/eyes/mouths
6Massive/expansive/sprawling
7Pallid/fleshy/knobby
8Powerful/burrowing/rending
9Plantlike (roots, rhizomes, bulbs)
10Slimy/oozing/mucous
11Sporting tentacles/tendrils/feelers/ antennae
12Worm-like/slug-like/grub-like Combine 2-3 of the above with the vaguely humanoid form it once had as a Green Lord. The PCs have encountered some horrors in the Great Wood—some crinwin oozing silver slime and wasps with hallucinatory venom. I already decided that these things were corrupted by the Things Below, based on the themes of "insanity/chaos/ruin/quicksilver" and "corruption/disease/poison/vermin." Now I'm thinking that a Fomoraij is behind them. I keep the two themes, and roll a constraint of "confinement." For its lair, I roll "a series of underground streams, rivers, lakes, all teeming with horrors." For its form, I get "too many limbs/eyes/mouths," and "a swarm of smaller things" and "slimy/oozing/mucous." I ponder, and come up with: Yaarowslow the Many is trapped away on an island in an underground lake, fed by tiny rivulets of groundwater. It has transformed its flesh into an oozing, silvery nest, host to an endless swarm of skittering and buzzing horrors that serve as its eyes and hands and mouths. For centuries, the vermin have burrowed through stone to reach the surface. It has recently succeeded, and its silvery spawn now taint a spring deep in the Great Wood.

Artifacts

Various Treasures

  • Preserved chimera corpse, mounted for display (immobile, Value 1)
  • A pouch of shimmering powder ◇ ( doses, Value 2 to the right buyer); ingest a dose and start compulsively telling secrets to those around you
  • A polished slice of agate the size of a dinner table, bringing nightmares and dark thoughts to those who linger in its presence (immobile, Value 2)
  • A pair of what seem to be rubies (Value 3) set as the eyes of a horrible statue; they're actually red crystals and impart a cruel bloodlust on whoever holds them.

Bloodshot Amber

◇ each, magical

fist-sized gems, riddled with red-black intrusions.

When you drip blood onto an amber, name a manifest spirit in your presence and roll

+CHA. If the spirit has an intact tether, roll with disadvantage. On a 10+, the spirit is imprisoned in the amber;

on a 7-9, the amber starts to suck the spirit in, but it may yet fight back; on a 6-, the spirit wrests free and the amber shatters.

When you imprison a spirit in a bloodshot amber, it is wracked with pain and cannot manifest. Only one spirit can be imprisoned at a time. When you touch the amber with bare skin, you can communicate with the spirit within and release it if you want (even if you weren't the one who imprisoned it).

When a bloodshot amber is found, it likely contains a spirit that has been trapped in agony for centuries.

Dream-Lily Pool

A raised pool of clear water. A strange lily grows within, its tendrils pouring over the lip and coating the floor, hiding bleached bones. The tendrils rustle when living things approach, and a gentle wind promises prophecy and wisdom.

When you let a lily-tendril slither under your skin, take damage (ignores armor) and fall deeply asleep. Roll +WIS: on a 10+, you dream of wisdom—ask the GM any question and get a helpful, honest answer, though one cloaked in symbolism; on a 7-9, you dream of wisdom (as on a 10+) but also of hunger, desperation, and want—mark a debility when you wake; on a 6-, you are trapped in endless nightmare—mark two debilities and you shall not wake unless you find an exit or someone rescues you from without.

Regardless, your dreams are clearly visible in the waters of the pool. You get the sense that something else may be watching, too.

  • Unbound wraiths, like jackals sniffing around a butcher shop

Infected Servant

Fomoraij sometimes ensnare beings of the world above, capturing them, luring them in with promises of power, or compelling the weak-willed to attend them.

They then infect the victim, turning them into extensions of their will.

Start with a base creature, and then pick or roll for the nature of the infection, the means of control, and up to 3 gifts. Interpret them based on what you know about its master. Update the base creature's tags, stats, qualities, instinct, and moves accordingly.

Infection
1-2A bug/worm/vine, burrowed into their brain/spine
3-4A foul substance, injected/inhaled/ imbibed
5-6A fungus/plant, growing under their skin
7-8A spirit possessing their body, bound by intricate scars/tattoos
9-10A mass of vermin/a single horror, eating them from the inside out
11-12One or more shards of red crystal embedded in their skull
Means of Control
1-2By crushing their will
3-4By eating away at what they once were
5-6By filling them with euphoria/ pleasure
7-8By promising them power/immortality/revenge/a cure/etc.
9-10By wracking them with pain
11-12Roll twice, or make up something new If you like, roll for the extent of control.
Gift
1A reach, near, or far attack (tentacles, spitting globs, flung spines, etc.)
2A terrifying presence; an ability to spread panic/fear/horror
3An area attack (cloud of poison, blast of nettles, psychic scream, etc.)
4An amorphous/malleable/ shapechanging form
5An ongoing link to its master, an ability to share senses/thoughts/ words/power
6A way to spread its infection and/ or its master's influence (intentionally, incidentally, or both)
7Camouflage/stealth/ability to avoid notice
8Exceptional strength/speed/ferocity/deadliness
9Influence/control over the minds of others
10Minions/servants/ability to send out extensions of itself
11Mobility (jumping, flying, climbing, burrowing, squeezing, etc.)
12Unnatural resilience/vigor/durability Should a PC or an important NPC become infected by a Fomoraij (or one of their servants), write it up as a threat (an affliction). If a PC willingly becomes an infected servant, consider creating a playbook insert, using the Thrall for inspiration (see Book I, Thrall). I want a big scary servant of Yaarowslow the Many, a hagr that stumbled on the tainted spring. I roll "a foul substance, injected/ inhaled/imbibed" and think of Yaarowslow's silvery slime. I get "by eating away at what they once were" as a means of control, and imagine the silver slime corrupting it from the inside, replacing its blood and organs and overwhelming its mind with voices and delusions. For gifts, I pick "unnatural resilience/vigor/durability" and "a way to spread its infection and/or its master's influence." I then roll for a third gift and get "an area attack." "Typical" Fomoraij

Solitary, large, magical, cunning, cautious, terrifying

HP 26; Armor 4 (resilience), 0 vs. bronze
Damage mighty blows (hand, close, forceful)
Special qualities can sense anything that happens in its lair (but might not think to)
Instinct [Use constraint as a guide]
HP 24; Armor 5 (resilience, lacks organs), 2 vs. bronze (thick hide)
DAMAGE spurts of corrosive slime (hand, close, area, messy, 1 piercing)
SPECIAL QUALITIES largely immobile, hive-mind connection with its vermin swarms; half damage from stabbing or smashing
INSTINCT to escape

  • Overawe anyone in its presence
  • Twist, mutate, or transform living things with a gesture
  • Wield nearby plants and beasts as extensions of itself
  • Regenerate wounds from anything but orichalcum
  • > Overwhelm foes with horror and thousands of squealing voices
  • > Infect minds and bodies with silvery slime
  • > Wield its swarms and its silvery slime as an extension of itself
  • > Regenerate wounds from anything but orichalcum

No Fomoraij is truly typical; use the stats above as a starting point. A Fomoraij's constraint dictates its instinct. Add or change its tags, stats, special qualities, and moves based on the themes, constraint, lair, and forms that you've identified.

Yaarowslow the Many is a mass of flesh that oozes infectious silvery slime, and the host of skittering, slithering, and buzzing horrors that serve as an extension of its will.
