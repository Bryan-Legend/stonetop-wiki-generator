The Green Lord’s Tomb

Sajra the swyn’s lair · 1½ days into the Great Wood · Book I pp. 371–377

Green Lords · Crinwin · Folk glyphs · Fae

Truth

A dome-tomb of the Green Lords, mostly intact, wearing a hill and three old oaks. Forest Folk left offerings here. A Fae named Thornthumb lived in the outer halls for centuries until last autumn, when Sajra the swyn took his mind, his hoard, and the tomb. Sajra collects beautiful things — including children.

Inner vault still holds a mummified Green Lord, warded by loyal Fae. Sajra wants that vault. The kids are in the shrine. Pryder is not yet enthralled.

“The hill looks like a hill until the oaks, and the oaks look like oaks until the singing. Two children’s voices. Too many mouths.”

This table

Book names: Tiwlip (Woods child, last winter), Wynfor (Stonetop raid), Pryder (just taken). Swap for whoever this village has already lost.

  • Sven: children in a monster’s collection. Violence against the innocent is the whole wound.
  • Dagmar: 1½ days is ground he knows. Folk glyphs, wasp-hum, the unwholesome thing he’s been smelling in the Wood.
  • Geralt: a Maker tomb with a living hoard and a sealed inner vault. Power, if he can get past the Fae.
  • Homefront: crinwin have already raided. Leave Sajra and the next child goes before harvest.

Thornthumb

Displaced Fae · plant-sway · collector · oathbound to stay away from the tomb, Sajra, its servants, and its possessions. Instinct: to get his hoard back without breaking the oath.

If they have not met him, he can find them on the march or at the tree-line. He will help against Sajra. He will also swindle them. The book assumes they already promised not to steal from the hoard — treat that as a Fae oath if they said it.

“I am not allowed the hill. You are. Bring me what was mine and I will name every hole in the stone.”
“The snake thinks it is a king. It is a magpie with a long belly.”

Site rule — interior

True in A–G. White marble, plant-and-chimera scrollwork, often marred. Dark, but the stone throws light.

  • Damp, cool. Wooden archways that grew there. Crude graffiti in Green Lord script. Lazy wasps. Distant voices that might be kids, or crinwin.
  • Halls 10 feet wide, burial niches 8–24 per hall. No bodies. Some niches hold junk shrines (bones, potsherds, flawed Value 1 finery) — crinwin aping Sajra.
  • Burrows (S) on the map: 18 inches. Sajra and crinwin only. Kids or Thornthumb could squeeze. PCs and Pryder cannot.

Crinwin (~30)

Crinwin horde · small, hoarder, stealthy · HP 3 · Armor 1 (reflexes) · claws/rocks/choke (hand) · climbs like a squirrel · instinct: to covet. Half are trusted (replace cautious with zealous). New flee at half lost; trusted fight to death.

  • 4 sentries outside · 4–10 on Pryder in F · 3–6 asleep outside · 3–6 asleep in G · 10–15 off foraging · up to 5 doing crinwin stuff.
  • Alerted: 2–3 keep tabs; rest rally at A. In 10–20 minutes, once ~10 have gathered: mimic Tiwlip/Wynfor crying for help, lure to A, swarm.
  • Sajra / hoard / kids threatened: nearby crinwin alarm and attack.
  • Unaware: eating, nest-work, fighting over junk, bullying, mimicking Sajra or the kids.

Wasps (crinwin.html): swarm = (ignores armor) and writhing; anything else is Defy Danger +CON. Each later swarm hit steps the die up (max d10). Flee: roll +CON; 7+ escape and pick 1 (10+) or 2 (7–9): take swarm damage · mark a debility · run into another danger.

Sajra

Hill

Approach Exterior
“A wide low hill in last year’s grass. Three bare oaks. Briar. Worked stone in the dirt if you look. Children singing — the same two voices, over and over, from too many throats.”
  • Ask: Dagmar, you know this song — what is it? Geralt, how do you know that’s crinwin and not the kids?
  • Folk glyphs on the surrounding trees, faint: reverence, premnitsi (“the receivers”), istina (“truth,” or… “oracle?”).
  • Rotting cabbage, bad eggs. Hum that isn’t bees. Papery nests in the oaks.

Sentries

4 on watch in the oaks, often “singing.” asleep in nests. others outside doing crinwin stuff.

  • Notice PCs: stop singing, bird-call. Others hide. Sleepers wake and stay put.
  • Poke around: watch, sneak closer, keep bird-calling.
  • Try to enter: wolf-howls. Answers from the Wood. Inside goes on alert. ~30 min later crinwin come back.
  • Attack sentries: flee into canopy, or into the tomb. Keep hounding → howls.
  • Molest a nest: crinwin burst, wasps swarm, daze/confound, flee.

Ways in

  • Main (north): briar ditch, 15-foot arch half-full of soil. Cut the briar, or take scratches and risk a nest. → A.
  • Secret (west): 30 feet past the tree line, under a boulder, glamour and brush. Tight with a light load. Trapdoor T in D. Sajra does not know.
  • Burrows: 18 inches, only when you’re on top of them. Not for PCs.

Inner tomb

Linear Fae wards

Below G. The book leaves this for later; the moves live here so you are not flipping chapters. Kids first is enough for a session.

Antechamber

“Hexagon, twenty feet. Featureless white. No soot. No graffiti. Your iron hums like a lie.”
  • Iron or steel on them: the way to Humility will not open. Leave it here, or go back up.
  • No fight. The ward is the door.

Hall of Humility

When you enter the Hall of Humility, choose 1:

  • Tell a moment that left you humbled and small before the natural world → proceed to Gratitude.
  • Tell a personal triumph, pride, self-satisfaction → you exit into the antechamber.

Skip, force, mock it: the hall puts them back in the antechamber, iron or no. Repeat until they choose.

Hall of Gratitude

Shifting tableau: ancient people living and learning at the feet of tall magnificent beings. When you enter the Hall of Gratitude, roll +WIS.

  • 10+: witness someone learning a skill now taken for granted — describe it — then Offerings.
  • 7–9: you are living one of those lives. Describe the task for the Makers, and how you know it isn’t yours. Mark dazed. Offerings.
  • 6−: savagery, sickness, hunger, want. Tell a hardship you suffer most cruelly. Mark all three debilities. Tell how you find yourself. Offerings.

Hall of Offerings

“Two of them. Twelve feet, wood-skin, goat legs, antlers, no mouths, three white eyes. They plant those trunk-arms and swing forward. Creaking wood. Something that was on your left is on your right.”

Almtakers · group, large, Fae, magical, organized · HP 14 · Armor 5 (resilience, skill), 2 vs iron · powerful blows w/ disadvantage (close, reach, forceful) · Fae nature, telepathic, cannot lie · warp local time and space · instinct: protect its master.

Almtaker 1
Almtaker 2

  • Know Things: interesting — Folk called them Priemnitsi, “Receivers,” for a great oracle. Useful — gifts new since the Green Lords (or vivid memories of such) land well. This place is out of time.
  • Deceive them (even with true words): roll +WIS. 10+ they don’t see it; 7–9 they probe — come clean or double down; 6− they have you.
  • Moves: lock eyes and teleport you away · act in tandem · require an oath · wasting curse on oath-breakers.

  • Portal here to the false afterlife — only if they let you, or you force it.

Burial vault & afterlife

Vault is sealed — no physical door. Body, canopic jars, grave goods inside. Spirit lives in a Fae domain: dusty halls, feral gardens, discarded wealth, surly bound Fae. The Green Lord’s soul swings from boredom to manic fits; the Fae on hand cobble threadbare glamour of half-remembered deeds. Theme: promises, deals, loopholes. Time does not behave.

Mummified Green Lord · HP 24 · Armor 4 (wards), 0 vs fire · mighty blows (hand, close, forceful) · tethered to jars, senses the whole tomb · instinct: preserve its glorious afterlife.

Green Lord
  • It does not want to leave the dream. Threaten the afterlife, the jars, or the corpse, and it will inhabit the body.
  • Regenerates even from fire unless the canopic jars are destroyed.
  • Twist living things with a gesture. Wield plants and beasts in the tomb as limbs.

Payoffs

  • Kids out: fever, silk, Sajra’s voice still in two of them for a while. Homefront: a debt, a feast, a fear that the Wood takes children.
  • Sajra dead: trusted crinwin may fight on; new ones scatter and become a pest threat. Thornthumb wants in. The spring-spirit weeps and thanks them in the same breath.
  • Sajra fled: it will collect somewhere else. Next season, another child, or a hunter who doesn’t come back.
  • Hoard: Value 4 if they can move it. Oath to Thornthumb if they swore one. Fence it in Gordin’s Delve and you buy a different kind of trouble.
  • Left the inner tomb: correct for a first pass. It waits. Geralt will think about it.
  • Collapsed G: inner tomb buried with them or without them. Doom as written.
“The Wood is louder on the way home. Every bird is a bird until it isn’t. Behind you the hill is a hill again, if you don’t look at the stone in the grass.”
