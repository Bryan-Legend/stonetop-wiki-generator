# Dice sounds

`dice-1.mp3` … `dice-4.mp3` — four takes of wooden dice thrown on a wooden
table, converted to mono MP3 and peak-normalised from the original FLACs.

- **Source:** "Wooden dice on wodden table roll" by **Wuzzy**,
  <https://opengameart.org/content/wooden-dice-on-wodden-table-roll>
- **Licence:** [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (public domain dedication — no attribution required; credited here anyway)
