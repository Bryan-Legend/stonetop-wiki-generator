附录A：世界诸纪

Most events are listed in the order they occurred, but events in italics and starting with?

are approximate, their specific placement unclear. Did construction begin on the Makers' Roads before or after the Tempest Lords' capital sank? Did Brennan become Marshedge's marshal before or after the Forest Folk disappeared? That's for you and your group to decide, if it matters.

太古纪

Eons ago, over a span beyond reckoning.

  • Light fills the void; the Things Below were already there
  • 初代造物者（First Creators）（太古之力）意识到自身的存在

？ 最初的 大精魂（太古之力） 兴起

  • 地底之物煽动创造者的自我，怂恿开战
  • 初代造物者锻造世界，把地底之物困在其中
  • Tcaventes, Shackle and Key, is set to guard the Utterwell ? The gods do many legendary things

？ 最早的诸族演化而出，或被创造

匠造者时代

Millennia ago, over a span of centuries (at least).

  • 风暴领主（Tempest Lords）文明兴起（风暴领主）
  • 风暴领主建立帝国
  • 其他匠造者群体兴起（匠造者）
  • Tempest Lords found "Northernmost" (the Ruins at Road's End, 惧河)
  • 风暴领主的故土沉没，帝国崩解；其他匠造者势力增长

? Construction of the Makers' Roads begins 匠造者之路 ? Green Lords breed the Fae ? Manmarchers come from across the Dread River

  • 风暴捕手（Stormcatcher）（废塔）在荒原（the Flats）立起她的塔
  • 妖精反叛！绿领主求助于地底之物
  • Stone Lords destroy the heart of the Green Lord's territory, creating what is now Ferrier's Fen
  • Siege of Vor Svetelik; Green Lords retreat, become Fomoraij
  • Stone Lords' servants start to plot rebellion and embrace the Things Below

Were the civilizations of the South founded during the Time of the Makers, or even before? Or perhaps during the Time of the Barrow Builders?

灾变时代

Centuries ago, over a span of decades.

  • Stone Lords' servants rebel, unleash the Things Below, sink Dulenn and create Blackwater Lake ? "Northernmost" overrun by horrors from the Dread River
  • 石领主被叛军猎杀
  • Teirstered falls, becomes Three Coven Lake
  • 难民开始奔向风暴捕手的塔

? Howling Curse causes Rime Lord civilization to nearly collapse 霜领主 ? Volcanic eruptions, Moratia Rubra destroyed, Forge Lord society collapses 熔炉领主

  • 更多难民抵达风暴捕手的塔
  • 幸存的霜领主自我牺牲，以遏制嚎叫诅咒
  • 风暴捕手的塔陷落

坟冢建造者时代

Centuries ago, over a span of generations.

  • 为纪念自由战士筑起第一批古冢
  • Emanations of the Things Below roam freely, are worshipped/appeased
  • 军阀/巫王争权，筑起更为繁复的古冢
  • 围绕三巫湖、路尽废墟等地爆发战事
  • 难民、奴役、人祭，处处都是糟心事
  • 坟冢建造者的臣民推翻/抛弃/囚禁他们的暴君
  • Certain bands of Barrow Builders flee into the Labyrinth

当今时代

始于数百年前。

  • 地底之物的力量消退（原因不明）
  • Barrow Builder culture gives rise to Hillfolk culture ? Marshedge founded 泽畔 ? Stonetop founded 石顶村
  • 地区安定下来，商路开辟
  • 戈丁矿场（Gordin's Delve）有人定居，铸坊（Foundry）建立（戈丁矿场）

近时记忆

大约最近十年。

? Brennan and the Claws start raiding 泽畔

  • 地震变得更为频繁
  • 森林之民（森林之民）消失

？ 布伦南受雇为泽畔的统帅

  • Crinwin, nailadd, etc. grow in numbers
