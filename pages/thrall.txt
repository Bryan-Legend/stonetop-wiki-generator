H3	Favor
TRACK	thrall-favor	Favor	3	Favor
P	Your Favor starts at 0 and can go no higher than 3. When you <b><i>have 3 Favor and would gain another</i></b>, reduce your Favor to 0 and choose 1:
B	Ask a question of your master and gain advantage on your next roll to act on the answer.
B	Gain a new Mark of your choice. Then ask the GM to choose a Mark that you don't have, and cross it off—you can never gain it.
H3	Urges
P	When <b><i>the GM compels you to act on your impulse</i></b>, gain 1 Favor if you act as bidden. If you resist, roll +WIS: <b>on a 10+</b>, your actions are your own; <b>on a 7-9</b>, choose 1:
B	Struggle for control until something/one snaps you out of it
B	Start acting as compelled, putting yourself or an ally in a spot before you regain control
B	Harm yourself (d6 damage, ignores armor) to regain control
P	<b>On a 6-</b>, you come to your senses later, having done gods-know-what.
P	When you <b><i>act on your impulse without being compelled to do so</i></b>, and your actions cause you or your allies trouble, gain 1 Favor.
H3	Dark Succor
P	When you <b><i>are dying or killed outright</i></b>, your master intercedes on your behalf. You will recover, here and now or at a time and place of the GM's choosing. Then, roll +Favor: <b>on a 10+</b>, choose 1; <b>on a 7-9</b>, choose 2; <b>on a 6-</b>, all 3 apply.
B	Gain a new Mark of the GM's choice
B	Cross off a Mark that you don't have—you can never gain it
B	Your master gives you a task; until you complete it, your Favor stays at 0.
P	Regardless, reset your Favor to 0.
H3	Unholy Vessel
P	When you <b><i>would gain a Mark but there are none left to gain</i></b>, your humanity is utterly lost. You become a threat in the GM's control. Make a new character.
H2	Marks
P	When you first gain this insert, the GM will choose 1 Mark for you, based on your master's nature. Gain more when a move tells you.
