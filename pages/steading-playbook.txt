H2	Content
P	Keep this in sync with the GM playbook. Review it at the start of each session.
P	When <b><i>anyone calls "time out,"</i></b> play stops. Step out of character, check in with each other, maybe take a break. Discuss what's wrong, player-to-player.
P	If <b><i>content was included that shouldn't have been</i></b>, acknowledge the mistake, fix the fiction, and move on.
P	If <b><i>someone realizes they need content to be excluded, veiled, or handled in a particular way</i></b>, then update the lists. Clarify specifics, now or later, but don't ask reasons. Fix the fiction. Check in with the player(s). When everyone is ready, move on.
