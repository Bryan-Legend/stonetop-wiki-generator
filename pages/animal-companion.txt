ENDSHEET
H3	Order Followers
P	When you <b><i>direct them to make a move and they do so</i></b>, roll +1 instead of +STAT if any of their tags apply (or +2 if they're also <i>exceptional</i>). If no tags apply, roll +0. If any of their tags would get in the way, roll with disadvantage.
P	When they are <b><i>without orders</i></b> or they <b><i>act on their own initiative</i></b>, the GM says what they do and how it goes.
H3	Strengthen Your Bond
P	When you <b><i>pay their cost</i></b> and you haven't done so recently, they hold +1 Loyalty (max 3).
P	Spend their Loyalty 1-for-1 to have them:
B	Overcome their fear to do as you say
B	Resist acting on their instinct/tags/traits
B	Do something they don't want to do (as long as it's not abhorrent or suicidal)
H3	Loyal to the End
P	When <b><i>your companion is at 0 HP</i></b>, roll +0, with advantage if it holds Loyalty: <b>on a 10+</b>, it'll be fine once it regains any HP; <b>on a 7-9</b>, it gets the <i>injured</i> tag; <b>on a 6-</b>, it's injured and will die soon unless someone saves it.
